Setting up Grower Contracts & Field GPS Plot Mapping

Guide to digital outgrower farmer onboarding, high-precision GNSS polygon boundary walking, and sugarcane supply agreements.

1. Overview & Operational Context

Outgrower management is the cornerstone of raw cane supply for sugar mills in Kenya (such as Chemelil, Muhoroni, Sony Sugar, and Mumias). The Synergy Bravo ERP Field Mobile application allows field extension officers to execute 100% digital grower onboarding, block mapping, and contract signing directly on mobile tablets even in remote areas without cellular signal.

2. Step-by-Step Onboarding & Registration Workflow

  1. Access Officer Module: Open the Synergy Field App on your Android/iOS tablet and select Grower Management > Register New Outgrower.
  2. Grower Identification & Financial KYC: Record grower full name, National ID number, primary contact number, and payout channel (M-PESA B2C mobile wallet or Kenya Commercial Bank account number).
  3. High-Precision Polygon GPS Walk: Stand at the perimeter corner of the cane block. Enable high-accuracy GNSS/GPS mode. Walk along the farm boundary to record polygon vertices. The app automatically calculates acreage (in hectares/acres) and flags any land boundary overlaps with neighboring contracted plots.
  4. Crop & Variety Assignment: Select the cane variety (e.g. KEN82-493 for high sucrose, CO421 for drought resistance, or N14), ratoon cycle status (Plant Crop, Ratoon 1, Ratoon 2), planting date, and projected harvest window (14-18 months).
  5. Digital E-Signatures & Contract Generation: Generate the standard Kenya Sugar Board outgrower supply agreement. Capture the grower's electronic signature on device and print a Bluetooth thermal copy for the grower's record.

3. Offline Queue & Sync Protocol

When operating offline in remote field blocks, all GPS polygons, photos, and signed contracts are stored locally in an encrypted SQLite mobile database queue. As soon as the device connects to 3G/4G or Wi-Fi, the background sync engine pushes all pending records to the central ERP database without data loss.

Weighbridge & Mill ⏱️ 7 min read

Weighbridge Scale Calibration, Gross & Tare Ticket Sync

Technical setup for connecting load cells, automated gross and tare weight locking, driver RFID trip tickets, and weight calculation formulas.

1. Hardware Integration Setup

The Weighbridge Gateway software interfaces directly with Avery Weigh-Tronix, Mettler Toledo, and Rice Lake weight indicators via serial COM ports or TCP/IP socket listeners.

2. Full Weighbridge Workflow

  1. Gate Entry & Trip Authorization: Driver taps RFID card or scans barcode at Gate 1. System verifies active harvest permit and truck license plate.
  2. Gross Scale Measurement: Vehicle moves onto Scale Platform 1. Click Lock Gross Weight. The system records gross tonnage from the load cell sensors.
  3. Cane Yard Discharge & Sampling: Truck proceeds to the unloader / cane yard feeder table. Laboratory sampler extracts juice sample for Brix & Pol analysis.
  4. Tare Weight Capture: Empty truck returns to Scale Platform 2. System captures tare weight and automatically computes: Net Cane = Gross Weight - Tare Weight - Deducted Trash %.
  5. Thermal Ticket & Barrier Release: A QR-coded thermal weighbill prints automatically and opens the exit barrier gate.

Weighbridge & Mill ⏱️ 6 min read

Sucrose Extraction Rates & Sugar Mill Laboratory Quality Logs

Walkthrough for logging Brix %, Pol %, purity, fiber %, and calculating Estimated Recoverable Crystal (ERC) for quality bonuses.

Laboratory Quality Control Workflow

Juice samples collected at first expressor roller are tagged with the corresponding delivery batch number.

  • Enter Brix hydrometer reading and refractometer temperature corrections into the Lab Quality Module.
  • Record polarimeter Pol reading; system automatically computes Sucrose % in juice.
  • ERC formula: ERC = (Pol % * 0.98) - (Non-Pol Solids * 0.4).
  • High-sucrose deliveries automatically trigger quality incentive bonuses added to grower settlement accounts.

Financials & Payroll ⏱️ 6 min read

Processing Grower Settlements, Cane Deductions & M-PESA Payroll

Calculate grower gross earnings from delivered cane tonnage and automatically net against fertilizer advances and transport deductions.

Settlement Calculation Flow

  1. Go to Financials > Grower Payroll & Settlements.
  2. Select billing cycle date range (e.g. 1st - 15th of the month).
  3. System automatically aggregates all weighbills delivered by the grower during the period.
  4. Automatic deduction offsets applied:
    • Seed cane & fertilizer advances
    • Tractor transport freight charges per km/tonne
    • Harvesting gang labor wages
    • Cooperative development levy (1%)
  5. Click Execute Bulk Payment to push payments via M-PESA B2C API or direct KITS bank transfer.

Troubleshooting ⏱️ 3 min read

Troubleshooting Hardware Scale Offline Mode & Data Sync

Quick steps to resolve serial COM port disconnections, local database queue backlog, and network reconnection sync.

Troubleshooting Steps

  1. Check USB-to-Serial adapter LED indicators on the weighbridge terminal PC.
  2. Open Weighbridge Sync Status from taskbar tray; confirm COM port setting matches device manager (e.g. COM3, 9600 baud rate).
  3. If internet connection drops, tickets automatically buffer to SQLite local storage. Do not restart terminal PC until queue reaches 0 after network reconnects.
